# Consolidation: what this project established

Phase 7. Every number here traces to a file under `results/`; the tables are
generated by `code/reporting/build_results_tables.py` and live in
[RESULTS_TABLES.md](RESULTS_TABLES.md).

---

## The claim

**Self-supervised pretraining on a 9,480-spectrum NMR corpus does not beat
classical machine learning on small clinical cohorts, and the binding constraint
is the number of independent studies in the corpus, not the number of spectra.**

All three gates of the PI's decision tree were reached and answered. The project
has a terminal result.

## The three results, in the order they should be written

### 1. Classical ML wins, and it is not an artefact

24 of 24 cohort x label-budget cells, mean gap +0.055, at every budget including
k=5. Established across all four transfer modes the plan specified, so it is a
statement about the architecture, not only about frozen representations.

The result is *not* explained by:
- **a broken corpus** — it reproduces on the rebuilt, verified corpus (the
  original pipeline destroyed absolute chemical shift; the rebuild restored the
  lactate CH₃ to 1.325 against a true 1.330, span 0.117 ppm)
- **freezing the backbone** — `last_block` is −0.0087 and `full` −0.0104 against
  the frozen probe; deeper adaptation is *worse*, the signature of overfitting
- **a weak pretext task** — pretraining beats random init by +0.057 in 64 of 72
  cells

### 2. Diversity, not volume, is the binding constraint

At constant effective diversity, a 7.5× increase in spectra buys nothing
(+0.0016 per doubling, p=0.33 at k=all). Diversity keeps paying across the entire
reachable range.

The sharpest single number: **the corpus has an effective study count of 4.09**,
not 12. Seven of its twelve studies hold 1–76 rows between them while one holds
57%. "We have twelve studies" was never true in the sense that matters.

A corollary worth reporting: balanced subsampling *raises* effective diversity
above the full corpus (`fixed2000_k12` reaches 6.88 on a quarter of the rows). More
data is not automatically more diverse.

### 3. Synthetic data cannot currently be validated

Median R² = 0.25 for a 94-component GISSMO + lipid basis against real serum CPMG
spectra. Three quarters of the signal is unmodelled, so synthetic spectra are
sparse where real ones are dense and a discriminator separates them perfectly.
This is a limit of the public basis, not a proof about synthetic data in general.

## Phase 6: capacity was measured, and helps too little

Run as a two-sided sweep, 1.82 M to 23.52 M parameters around the 4.0 M baseline,
12 runs. See [PHASE6_capacity_verdict.md](PHASE6_capacity_verdict.md).

| params | k=10 | k=all |
|---|---|---|
| 1.82 M | 0.574 | 0.644 |
| 3.99 M (baseline) | 0.590 | 0.666 |
| **10.54 M** | **0.603** | **0.678** |
| 23.52 M | 0.598 | 0.675 |
| **classical ML** | **0.654** | **0.721** |

Capacity helps — largest beats smallest in 6 of 6 targets — but at +0.008 to
+0.009 per doubling, and the curve is flat from ~10 M onward. The best
architecture still loses every cell, by 0.046 at k=all.

**This section previously argued the sweep did not need running**, on the grounds
that the encoder already overfits so more capacity would hurt. That was wrong:
smaller models are *worse*, not better. Fine-tuning overfits because it adapts
4 M parameters on 10–40 labelled spectra; pretraining fits them on 8,545
unlabelled ones, and those are different regimes. The conclusion survives, but it
now rests on measurement rather than on a mistaken inference.

## Limitations, stated plainly

1. **Corpus diversity.** Effective 4.09 studies. Every conclusion about scaling is
   bounded by an axis this corpus can only traverse from 1.57 to 6.90.
2. **Cohort size.** 40–142 samples, by design — small n is the object of study,
   but it means episode sd is 0.05–0.09 and single-cell differences below ~0.045
   (the measured single-run noise floor) are not interpretable.
3. **CPMG only.** The corpus is CPMG by upstream selection. Conclusions may not
   transfer to other acquisition types, and it is why the lipid basis contributed
   nothing.
4. **One architecture family.** A 4-layer, 192-dim transformer encoder. Patch
   size and pooling were swept; depth and width were not.
5. **Absolute referencing spread.** ~0.05 ppm between studies remains after the
   rebuild. Judged acceptable natural variation, not corrected.
6. **G3 is route-limited.** The generative route was argued against rather than
   tested; the argument (a generator cannot raise effective study count) is sound
   but is reasoning, not measurement.

## What would change the answer

Stated as recommendations, since this is the useful output of a negative result:

- **More independent studies**, not more spectra. The diversity effect had not
  saturated at the limit of what this corpus can reach.
- **A metabolite basis covering the serum metabolome**, which would make the
  synthetic route testable rather than foreclosed.
- **Fine-tuning at cohort sizes in the hundreds**, where adaptation has enough
  signal to not simply overfit.

## Methodological notes worth carrying into the write-up

Three defects were found and fixed during this work, all of which produced
*plausible* rather than obviously broken results. They are worth a methods
paragraph, because each would have changed a conclusion:

1. **The axis defect.** Per-spectrum re-referencing to the rightmost peak plus
   resampling onto each spectrum's own ppm range. Every spectrum looked normal
   alone; only between-row comparison exposed it.
2. **Selection bias from best-of-N checkpoints.** An apparent Barth "SSL win" was
   the best of 15 checkpoints; every objective's seed mean was below classical.
   Retracted twice on this project before the summariser was rewritten to report
   seed means.
3. **A crippled control.** The first fine-tuning design trained its own linear
   head, which underperformed the sklearn probe by 0.25 — fine-tuning would have
   "won" by 0.06–0.14 against it. Fixed by giving every arm an identical readout.

The general lesson: on this project, wrong code produced confident, publishable-
looking numbers rather than errors. Every headline number should be reproducible
from a committed result file.
